3. GAMMA BRANCH: MATHEMATICAL SCOPE AND DEPENDENCIES

The definition of F_k counts arbitrary distinct n>=0 true visits, rather
than assuming an anchored visit at n=0. The manuscript does not falsely
identify F_1 with Sigma. Both limiting Hausdorff and packing dimensions
are treated. The stronger operational statement is a uniform finite J
such that F_J is covered by countably many compact sets of arbitrarily
small upper box dimension.

The extraction does not require a uniform bound on the span. Its uniform
quantity is the number J of supplied distinct dyadic slopes. The full
finite proof retains the incidence bound, finite Ramsey refinements,
strict valuation ordering, unique minimum determinant term, canonical
slot-word argument, the five-vector obstruction over F_3, and Cramer
precision control. The error-tie deletion explicitly concerns nonzero
error coefficients at a finite least valuation. The original physical
exponent order is restored before checking successive gaps. The final
distance-overlap selection allows repeated distances within a row but
produces disjoint distance sets between selected rows.

The virtual anchor alpha is a rational 3-adic unit. Consequently X=alpha*x
need not belong to Sigma, and the finite-word proposition correctly
allows arbitrary ternary input. The choice q=b+1 precedes the requirement
that the extracted gaps be at least q. Ordinary addition has exactly r
lower residues and carry in {0,...,r-1}. The prefix-dependent center is
legal for the reproduced causal theorem. Fixing b low digits yields a
unique low representative because (3^b-1)/2+r-1<3^b. The one remaining
high digit lies in one translate of {0,1}; a full additive product in
F_3 therefore has product cardinality at most 2. This is the sharp
one-digit estimate used for Gamma, not the older two-translate estimate.

Conditional supports obtained from different longer-prefix atoms all
lie in the corresponding alphabets determined by the shorter prefix and
the same fingerprint. Their recombination is justified by the exact
full-Cartesian-product conclusion, not by an assumption that a single
input realizes every combination. Boundary incidences, fingerprint
entropy and the initial b digits give the stated finite-word bound.
The r-then-u parameter hierarchy makes the residual entropy costs vanish.
Each component's span is fixed before L tends to infinity.

All extraction coefficients are rational; the finite component data are
countable. Multiplication by alpha is a 3-adic isometry. Countable
stability of packing dimension therefore proves Gamma_H=Gamma_P=0.
The E2 theorem follows as a corollary. The finite-rank rational-unit
group remark preserves its rank dependence and proves the necessary
injectivity by the unit valuation; it does not assert a conclusion for
arbitrary collections of integer multipliers.

4. REAL BRANCH: SEPARATE COMPLETE ARGUMENT

The manuscript treats the full, untruncated real digit condition,
including every fractional digit. It explicitly distinguishes this
from Lagarias's floor-truncated set of dimension log_3(2). The claimed
result is dim_H E_R=dim_P E_R=0 and a countable compact upper-box cover.

The infinite extraction is independently stated for an arbitrary
infinite subset of nonnegative visit times. Compactness concerns the
dyadic unit slopes in Z_3, not the initial real point. At every finite
stage an infinite collection of exact stops would give distinct
nondegenerate finite-rank unit-equation solutions; distinct valuations
exclude vanishing subsums. Hence residual normalization can continue.
Finite coefficient approximations and a sufficiently close TRUE visiting
anchor preserve the terminal unit remainder. Strong cross-span
separation is used only here, where the infinite extraction supports it;
it is not imposed on the uniform finite-J theorem. Slopes can be dyadic
fractions and this is explicitly retained.

The real affine finite-word bound is uniform over all fixed shifts
beta_i in Z_3 and counts every locally admissible finite word, whether
or not infinitely extendable. It uses q=b+k, not q=b+1. The shifted
prefix center beta_i+3^E*u_i*[X]_j is causal. The low-representative
interval has at most two representatives, and the high k-digit sumset
is contained in two translates of Sigma_k. The full Kneser capacity
proof is included: a stabilizer coset has 3^h elements but meets the
two translates in at most 2^(h+1), forcing h<=1; quotient Kneser then
gives sum log|A_i|<=r log3+2^(k+1)log2. Its constants are translation
uniform. The conditional-support argument and overlapping-window chain
rule yield the stated s and boundary (R+b+k-1)log3.

For c_i=p_i/q_i>0 with 3 not dividing p_i*q_i, the exact floor error is
beta_i,L=floor(3^L*c_i*X)-c_i*floor(3^L*X). It lies in
q_i^(-1) Z intersect (-1,p_i/q_i), a fixed set of p_i+q_i-1 choices.
Every such shift belongs to Z_3. Reducing the integer part modulo 3^L
is valid because c_i is a 3-adic unit. Full admissible real fractional
tails are at most 1/2, so taking floors preserves the required 0/1
integer prefixes, including terminating cases. No reversed infinite
word or unread real tail is fed into the causal theorem.

The shift vector may change with the point and with L. The proof takes
a fixed finite union at each depth, which is exactly what the uniform
finite-word estimate permits. It does not assume one shift vector for
all depths. The real cell bound includes M+1 possible integer parts
and product(p_i+q_i-1), both constant for a fixed component. The digit
set is closed on bounded intervals, giving compact finite intersections.
The union is indexed only by integer visit times, integer exponents,
integer coefficient approximations, and M; no uncountable accumulation
point indexes it. The similarity 2^(-n0) preserves upper box dimension.
The order of limits fixes rational heights and spans before L->infinity,
then uses countable stability of packing dimension. The real packing
conclusion is therefore justified, not inferred from Hausdorff zero.
