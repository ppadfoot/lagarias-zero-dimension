ARITHMETIC AND P-ADIC DIMENSION AUDIT
The uniform finite incidence proof normalizes to a nondegenerate unit
equation after discarding finitely many valuation ties. Distinct powers
give distinct variable tuples; the rank and ESS solution bound are uniform
in the number of rows and in their heights. Pair Ramsey refinement gives
the strict Monge order and valuation-minimal coordinate minors. The next
Ramsey steps do not assume general position: a large all-zero augmented-
minor set would contradict that uniform incidence bound.

The determinant word, fixed minimum cofactor pairs, descending single-
repeat pattern and five-vector quotient obstruction are retained in full.
Five vectors with equal pair-determinant valuations in Q3^2 are impossible:
after choosing two as a basis, at most two additional nonzero unit ratios
can have distinct residues in F3. Cramer interpolation preserves unit
coefficients. The bad-row count correctly excludes zero-error terms from
finite valuation ties. Explicit Ramsey budgets and backward stage budgets
make J depend only on r,K,q. No precision-dependent iteration or bound on
the template spans has been smuggled into the finite claim.

The finite-word proposition allows all ternary input words, which is
essential for the virtual anchor. Ordinary carries lie in 0,...,r-1.
After low b digits are fixed, the sharper interval of length less than
3^b leaves one high-digit target translate and product capacity at most 2.
The nonlocal joint letters and conditional support recombination satisfy
the exact included container theorem. The boundary term (R+b)log 3 and
bad-edge coefficient eta are unchanged. Choosing fixed r, then finite u,
then J yields a countable compact cover of F_J by sets of box dimension
less than any prescribed delta. Rationality makes the indexing countable;
each span is fixed before digit length tends to infinity. Countable
stability of packing dimension proves both Gamma limits zero.

The finite-rank rational-unit-group remark remains valid. Unit ratios
replace dyadic multiplicative independence for injectivity of normalized

the result for arbitrary integer-multiplier families. The manuscript

REAL-PROOF AUDIT AND ADDED CAPACITY PROOF
The real argument deliberately keeps a TRUE visiting anchor. Its compact
infinite-slope extraction yields successive valuation gaps tending to
infinity. Infinite exact stops would yield infinitely many nondegenerate
finite-rank unit-equation solutions, because the summand valuations are
distinct. Integer approximations to the finitely many limiting unit
coefficients and a sufficiently close true anchor change every selected
row only beyond its terminal valuation. The resulting slopes are positive
rational 3-adic units, even when an exponent difference is negative.

For the two-translate capacity estimate, H=Stab(T) has order 3^h. A coset
of H fixes the lowest k-h digits and meets each translate of Sigma_k in
at most 2^h points. A full H-coset in T therefore forces 3^h<=2^(h+1),
hence h<=1. In the quotient, the final sum and every partial sum have
trivial stabilizer: a stabilizer of a partial sum would also stabilize the
final sum. Iterating Kneser's aperiodic addition bound is therefore valid.
Together with n<=2^(n-1), it gives the displayed r log 3+2^(k+1)log 2
capacity uniformly in the target translates. The proof is complete and
uses the cited published sumset theorem, with no hidden digital premise.

Z3 by including them in a center determined by the source prefix. Its r
lower canonical residues still have carry 0,...,r-1. The two-translate
low-digit reduction and joint conditional supports agree with the exact
