Chosen mathematical core:
* Complete finite classification of unit-coefficient balanced digital
  planes attaining 3^m solutions, including the full target sets.
* Exact counts 24m-20 coefficient pairs and 28*2^m-48 full triples.
* Effective nonfullness at 3^m>4H^2 for a rational coefficient pair of
  height at most H which is not full at all depths.
* For Haar-almost every unit base b, E_b^(3)={0}, with one exceptional
  null set simultaneous in the initial point and all three visit times.

Proof dependencies are internal to the section. The finite classification
is proved by first unequal coefficient digits, injectivity of full target
lifts, coefficient coincidence, one-switch periodicity, and two-child
comparison. The metric proof supplies the complete 2^s prefix-cube
identity for every valuation gap s, the exact normalization of Haar
averages, the two-point residual parameter set, and the countable-ratio
and parity reductions. No projection theorem, energy estimate, zero-one
law, numerical recurrence, or prior generic countability claim is used.
The standard logarithm/exponential and integral-binomial facts on
principal 3-adic units are explained where used.

Scope: the theorem is about almost every base, not a prescribed base.
Complete-cube averaging is not a contraction on a fixed ratio fiber.
Neither the metric theorem nor rational-height finite-window bound proves
finiteness of the exceptional visits at the point 1 under base 2.
