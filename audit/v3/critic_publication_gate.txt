B. COUNTING RESULT
Read the recovered positive-density proof §§2–7 in full, independently checked KP Lemmas5/10 in the primary arXiv PDF, and read the complete new arithmetic/positive_density_section.tex. No mathematical defect found.
The only deep analytic input is the unconditional KP Lemma10 bound O(X/log^10 X) for small Carmichael values. A density-one statement without this rate would not suffice. The manuscript proves the needed order-product inequality itself. Bad-prime and powerful-part moment estimates leave ord_n(3)>=n^(1/3) outside O(X/log^10 X). Disjoint full rational orbits and elementary Cantor spacing then give summability over DISTINCT reduced denominators, not over rational points with multiplicity.
The finite sieve does not assume independence of composite divisibility events. Prime valuation coordinates are independent by CRT; decreasing events are positively associated. This yields only a product LOWER bound. The uniform reciprocal tail permits X→infinity at fixed Y, followed by Y→infinity, and proves a positive density for the sifted subfamily. It does not prove a density for all M_H.
The carry implication is one-way: zero dimension forces a reachable terminal simple cycle. A terminal cycle can coexist with a positive-dimensional component. Internal reduced denominator d>1 and 3∤d are indispensable. The positive finite-witness inclusion M_C⊂M_H is proved by separated repetition in the new section. Leading-digit gaps then give linear positive separation.
This determines the order requested in Lagarias Problem2 (journal1.11). It does not establish M_H~X/2, a sharp asymptotic for M_C, or a linear lower bound for M_C. The theorem is substantial enough to be a main result, without using the word fundamental as a substitute for mathematical comparison.
