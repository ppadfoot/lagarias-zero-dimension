Precisely: for large X the number of n≤X with
lambda(n)≤n exp(−(log log n)^3) is at most X/(log X)^10.
This is unconditional. The surrounding paper contains conditional
results too, but no such condition attaches to Lemma10. The section
does not invoke them. Lemma5's order inequality is re-proved inside
the section, so even that auxiliary citation is not a dependency.

lemma, not on a claimed new proof of FPS or on an unverified inference
from its conditional statements. The older archived audit records a
separate inspection of FPS and its corrigendum. In this publication
