# Provenance of version 2.0.0

Sole author: Timofei Iuzhakov. Version 2 combines the complete uniform 3-adic finite-visit/nesting argument with the complete real exceptional-set argument. The E2 conclusion from v1 remains a corollary. The shared finite causal-container appendix is byte-for-byte unchanged. The real proof includes its own extraction, capacity estimate, affine finite-word bound and floor transfer; it is not inferred solely from vanishing nesting constants.

`paper/merge_provenance.json` records the input source hashes and preservation checks. `audit/final_*` reports identify the exact final manuscript hashes they reviewed. New results are first included in v2 and are not attributed to the earlier release date.

Only completed results needed for this coherent paper are included. Separate results about integer quotients of ternary Cantor numbers have their own repository and citation. Raw private chat exports and unrelated research drafts are excluded.
