# Provenance of version 1.0.0

The author requested a standalone article from the research materials in the chat **«Аудит оценки Эрдёша 406»**, with **Timofei Iuzhakov as sole author**. The local collection contains 17 stages, seven subsequent verification packages, original articles, code, results, and review notes. The raw private chat and unrelated earlier manuscripts are not part of this public repository.

The dimension proof used here is the complete text of Stage 16, dated 5 November 2026, source SHA-256:

`1669addf65376940d0d568f57ea9d90a3661d56b73e70532ca7a8af0552a9ee8`

The English paper specializes its general setting to the exact set E2, retaining the full finite causal-container proof and the stronger packing conclusion. It omits separate results on affine rigidity, entropy plateaux, rational orbits, and ordinary exponents. The subsequent packages investigate those separate questions and explicitly do not replace the causal-container proof.

Earlier tentative reviews based only on conversation summaries lacked the full proof. They are superseded, within the stated scopes, by the full-source reviews recorded here. The present reviewers reread the proof and then the exact new English files, rather than relying on earlier PASS labels. Their reports identify the reviewed hashes and distinguish analytic arguments from finite checks. The original local paths in the reports document provenance and do not identify additional public repository files.

The manuscript date is a research date. The repository's actual initial public commit and version tag record this release's public availability. No earlier public release, DOI registration, external endorsement, or universal novelty determination is asserted.
