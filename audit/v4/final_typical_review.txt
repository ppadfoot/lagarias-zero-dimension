CLAIMS CHECKED

1. Theorem typical:main. For Haar-almost every lambda in 1+3 Z_3,
   Hausdorff, packing and both box dimensions of C_lambda are
   log_3(4/3). The class lambda=2 mod 3 has C_lambda={0}.
2. Corollary typical:canonical-measure. The fair-child measure has
   dimension (log_3 2)/3, a distinct and smaller value than its support.
3. Theorem typical:golden-gap. The isometric coding and sharp
   golden-ratio Assouad bound hold for arbitrary nonidentity 3-adic
   unit multipliers, with the residue-2 case handled separately.
4. Corollary typical:oscillation. The punctured upper and lower
   dimension limits at the identity use the two specified families.

FRESH PROOF CHECKS

A. Affine prefix counts and extendibility.
For lambda=1 mod 3, each allowed prefix has at least one child for
every affine target. Thus 1 <= R_n <= 2^n and every admissible finite
prefix extends. These facts justify the density logarithms, the block
choices and the identification of symbolic counts with actual covers.

B. Exact second moment (typical:l2).
The first differing digit has probability 2^(-k-1); matching leading
signs contribute a factor 1/2. The unit congruence for lambda then has
conditional probability 3^(-(n-k-1)). Summing the diagonal and these
off-diagonal contributions gives exactly

  E_lambda ||D_{lambda,n}||_2^2 = 3/2 - (1/2)(3/4)^n.

The increasing L2 martingale norms and their bounded integrals give
an L2 density for almost every parameter. No unsupported interchange
of exceptional parameter sets is needed.

C. Positivity (typical:positive).
The three branches with translations 0, 1 and -lambda have distinct
residues modulo 3. Their inverse-branch map preserves Haar measure
and is conjugate, by digit coding, to the full Bernoulli shift.
Nonnegativity in the self-similar density equation makes the zero set
forward invariant up to a null set; equality of Haar measures makes
it invariant. Ergodicity and integral one imply positivity almost
everywhere. Multiplicity of the fourth branch causes no problem.

D. Entropy limit (typical:entropy-limit).
For almost every parameter, D_n tends to a finite strictly positive
limit at almost every target. Hence log(D_n)/n tends to zero. The
uniform linear bounds from 1 <= R_n <= 2^n give dominated convergence;
the proof does not require integrability of log D. The limit is
H_n(lambda)/n -> log(4/3).

E. Homogeneous block measure (typical:block-measure).
The selected initial prefix is a unit and exists by extendibility.
At block j, the current prefix is adapted to already exposed data.
The next multiplier block L_j is fresh and uniform. Multiplication
by the current unit prefix permutes the residues modulo 3^q, so the
affine shift c_j+L_j P_j is conditionally uniform. The logarithmic
block counts are therefore independent and identically distributed
in the joint parameter-and-path experiment, conditional on the fixed
initial multiplier residue. The proof does not assert independence
after fixing the complete multiplier. Strong laws, the measurable
fiber measures and Fubini give the needed assertion for almost every
fixed parameter. For fixed q, the within-block loss is uniformly
bounded, so block depths suffice for the full lower local dimension.

The countable intersection over all block lengths is explicitly
intersected with the full-measure entropy-limit set. Taking q to
infinity therefore proves the homogeneous Hausdorff lower bound.
This is a genuine fiber argument, not an inference from almost every
affine target to the particular target zero.

F. Upper bounds for all four dimensions.
The unit-prefix first moment is (4/3)^(n-1). Markov's inequality and
Borel--Cantelli yield the required almost-sure upper exponential
rate. The identity N_n(C_lambda)=1+sum_{j=1}^n Q_j accounts for all
possible initial zero digits; its geometric-sum estimate gives the
upper box bound. The proof does not invoke countable stability of
box dimension. The Hausdorff lower bound then sandwiches packing and
both box dimensions at the stated value.

G. Canonical measure and causal golden-ratio coding.
At block length one, the logarithmic offspring count has mean
(log 2)/3; the same fiber argument gives the canonical measure's
exact local dimension. Its full support does not imply equality of
measure and support dimensions, and the source distinguishes them.

For lambda=1+3^k u, the relevant output predictor depends on the
already selected digits. Changing the digit at distance k changes
that predictor by a unit modulo 3. The causal recoding therefore
forces b_j b_{j+k}=0 and preserves first differing digits. Fibonacci
continuation counts give the uniform Assouad estimate. For
lambda=1-3^k, the digits are monotone on each residue class modulo k;
countability and uniform polynomial continuation counts give Assouad
dimension zero. The positive comparison family and its known exact
dimensions are attributed to the cited prior work. Together these
families prove the stated sharp punctured limits.
