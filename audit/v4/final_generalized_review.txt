ACCEPTED CLAIMS

With d = (log 2)/(2 log 3), the proof establishes

  dim_H E_* = dim_P E_* = Gamma_* = lim_J dim_P V_J = d,

and the upper convergence estimate

  limsup_J (dim_P V_J - d) log log J <= (log 2)^2/log 3.

For each fixed infinite family Lambda of distinct 3-adic units, it also
establishes

  dim_A (intersection_{lambda in Lambda} lambda^{-1} Sigma) <= d,

with equality for the stated family of all odd shifts. The accompanying
distinction between that fixed-family assertion and the variable-family
set E_* is correct: E_* has upper box and Assouad dimension log_3 2.

POINT-BY-POINT RECONSTRUCTION

1. Single and adjacent translate capacities.
   For one translate, two two-element residue projections cannot both
   occur modulo 3. There are at most two nonempty section pairs, and
   induction in the cyclic group gives |A||B| <= 2^q. For actual binary
   palettes A,B subset Sigma_q, multiplication by a unit and translation
   preserve the bound of two least-digit residues for the first palette.
   The union Sigma_q union (1+Sigma_q) has precisely all three least
   digits followed by Sigma_{q-1}. Thus there are at most four section
   pairs, giving 2^(q+1). The supplied equality example is valid, including
   q=1. No claim for arbitrary nonadjacent translates is substituted.

2. Pair-palette algorithm and causal replay.
   A heavy recorded source deletes at least T distinct pairs consisting
   of a future position and a letter. Simplicity of the directed graph
   ensures distinct targets for the witnessing edges. Actual future
   letters are never deleted. There are at most QN possible deletions,
   so the fingerprint size is at most floor(QN/T). A heavy source has no
   bad outgoing edges after deletion. At a light source the union over
   at most Q letters has fewer than QT potentially bad outgoing edges.
   Later shrinking preserves these assertions.

   Replay uses the entire fingerprint and the known input prefix. Earlier
   relations are therefore available. Membership of a source position in
   the fingerprint determines its branch of the algorithm. In the light
   branch the actual unrecorded letter is not needed. This gives precisely
   the stipulated conditional palette support, even when letters encode
   overlapping windows or depend on digits after their source position.
   The fingerprint counting and its binomial entropy estimate are valid
   for theta <= 1/2 and require no probability independence assumption.

3. Exact affine carry and overlap.
   With v=j+E_i and P=[X]_j, the residual coefficient
   D=(beta_i+3^{E_i}u_iP-[beta_i+3^{E_i}u_iP]_v)/3^v
   depends only on the prefix before j and the fixed parameters. Expanding
   modulo 3^(v+q) gives the displayed two-window formula. Its two lower
   summands are canonical representatives below 3^v; their ordinary carry
   is exactly either 0 or 1. This remains true for arbitrary 3-adic units
   u_i and arbitrary affine shifts beta_i. No assumption E_i >= q is
   needed: overlapping source and target windows are explicitly allowed.
   The resulting two translates are adjacent, as needed by the capacity
   lemma, and the relation has the required prefix dependence.

4. Entropy accounting.
   On good edges the sum of the two logarithmic palette sizes is at most
   (q+1)log 2. The crude bad-edge bound 2q log 2 and at most 2KR missing
   graph incidences give the stated weight estimate. They remain upper
   bounds when a shift exceeds the available window range. Conditional
   chain rule identifies the entropy of a window with the sum of the
   digit entropies conditioned on the full preceding input and F. The
   total deficit in q-fold coverage is exactly q(q-1), including L=q.
   Dividing the weight estimate by q and adding H(F) reproduces every
   term of sigma and the boundary term (R+q-1)log 2. The finite count is
   uniform in the affine shifts; it harmlessly counts prefixes without
   infinite admissible continuations as well.

5. Finite extraction and order of quantifiers.
   More than 2*3^(K-1) units leave more than 3^(K-1) in one nonzero
   residue class. A finite separating residue tree exists for any finite
   set of distinct 3-adic units. After suppressing unary vertices, at most
   K-1 branching vertices on every root-to-leaf path would imply at most
   3^(K-1) leaves. Choosing an anchor and leaves from side branches yields
   K distinct positive valuations. The normalization by the anchor is an
   isometry. Crucially, this argument works for every sufficiently large
   finite family, not merely some subsequence of an infinite family.
   The exponent and sufficient cardinality depend only on epsilon;
   the finite covering constant may depend on the extracted depths.

6. Localization and Assouad dimension.
   Inside a cylinder meeting the normalized intersection, the lower input
   and output digits are admissible. The remaining conditions are exactly
   the affine tail conditions in the finite lemma. That lemma supplies
   one constant independent of the cylinder's center and initial depth.
   The elementary estimate at L<q covers all remaining scales. Rounding
   arbitrary radii to adjacent powers of 3 only changes the constant.
   Thus the conclusion is genuinely an Assouad bound, not merely a global
   upper box bound. For an infinite fixed Lambda, one finite subfamily for
   each epsilon suffices; no uniform constant over all subfamilies and no
   countability assumption on Lambda are needed.

7. The transition to exact Gamma_* and packing limits.
   V_J is a countable union of compact intersections indexed by integer
   tuples. Packing dimension is countably stable, so family-dependent
   finite constants disappear before taking this union. There is no use
   of countable stability of box or Assouad dimension. E_* is contained
   in every V_J, including at zero. Every nonzero admissible multiplier
   is 1 modulo 3, and 1 itself is admissible since x belongs to Sigma.
   The known even-position Cantor set U lies in E_* and every V_J. Its
   cylinder count and fair measure give dimension d. These statements
   prove the exact values without commuting a limit with a supremum.

8. Quantitative rate.
   The selected K satisfies 2*3^(K-1) <= J-1 < J, so it is valid for
   every J-tuple. With q=floor((log K-5 log log K)/log 4) and T=2^q q^3,
   theta=q^(-3), the entropy term is O(q^(-3)log q), theta*q=q^(-2),
   and QT/K <= q^3/(log K)^5=O(q^(-2)). Therefore sigma=d+d/q+O(q^(-2)).
   The identities q~log K/log 4, log K~log log J, and
   d log 4=(log 2)^2/log 3 yield exactly the claimed limsup constant.
   Neither an asymptotic equality nor an optimal rate is asserted.

9. Sharpness and scope.
   For Lambda={1} union {1+3^(2k+1):k>=0}, sums of two binary digit
   strings produce no carries. Avoiding digit 2 for every odd shift is
   equivalent to all nonzero digit positions having the same parity.
   Hence the intersection is U union 3U, with Assouad dimension d.
   Finite Cantor integers belong to E_* and are dense in Sigma, giving
   its larger box and Assouad dimensions. The final remark correctly
   states that none of these assertions proves 1 notin E_2; indeed
   1 belongs to the generalized set E_*.
