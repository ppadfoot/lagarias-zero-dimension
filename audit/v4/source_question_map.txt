VERSION 4: SELECTED RESULTS AND SOURCE QUESTIONS
Date: 12 November 2026. Mathematical author: Timofei Iuzhakov.

This map identifies the questions and scope of the three selected additions.
A02, A03 and L18 are internal research identifiers, not conjecture numbers
assigned by Lagarias or Abram--Lagarias. The main text gives full proofs.
This is an attribution map, not an exhaustive worldwide priority survey.

1. GENERALIZED EXCEPTIONAL SET AND NESTING CONSTANT (A02)

Primary source:
W. C. Abram and J. C. Lagarias, Intersections of multiplicative translates
of 3-adic Cantor sets, J. Fractal Geom. 1 (2014), 349--390.
Definition 1.5; Problem 1.6; equation (1.14); Theorems 2.4 and 6.3.
https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/37215

Question: determine the dimension of E_*, where arbitrary positive integer
unit multipliers, including 1, replace the powers of two. Gamma_* is the
associated generalized finite-visit nesting limit.

Known input: the lower bound (log_3 2)/2 and positivity of E_* were already
proved in the cited work using Bolshakov's even-position construction.

Included in v4:
  dim_H E_* = dim_P E_* = Gamma_* = lim_J dim_P V_J = (log_3 2)/2.
The proof supplies the matching upper bound. It also proves a limsup upper
convergence rate with constant (log 2)^2/log 3 after multiplication by
log log J, and a sharp Assouad bound (log_3 2)/2 for every fixed infinite
family of distinct 3-adic unit multipliers.

Scope: the rate is not asserted optimal. The fixed-family Assouad result
does not apply to E_* itself: E_* has box and Assouad dimension log_3 2.
The generalized constant Gamma_* is distinct from the dyadic nesting
constants of the earlier article. In particular, 1 belongs to E_*.

Public proof: paper/generalized_exceptional.tex, gen:main and gen:assouad.
Review: audit/v4/final_generalized_review.txt.

2. TYPICAL MULTIPLICATIVE INTERSECTIONS (MEASURE-THEORETIC PART OF A03)

Primary context: Abram--Lagarias (2014), Section 2.4, the programme for
general 3-adic multipliers. This is a programme of parameter questions;
there is no source conjecture numbered A03 or quoted there with the
specific value log_3(4/3).

Included in v4: for Haar-almost every lambda in H=1+3 Z_3, the set
C_lambda=Sigma intersection lambda^{-1}Sigma and its unit part have
Hausdorff, packing, lower box and upper box dimensions log_3(4/3).
For the other unit residue class, C_lambda={0}.

Supporting results: an exact averaged collision formula, L2 density,
positivity, entropy saturation and the joint fresh-block/Fubini argument
provide a complete proof for the homogeneous target. The canonical
fair-child measure has full support and exact dimension (log_3 2)/3;
this is a measure statement, distinct from the larger support dimension.

Scope: Haar-almost every is not every parameter, every rational parameter,
or a Baire-category conclusion. The theorem does not determine the entire
exceptional spectrum and does not assert a typical Assouad dimension.

Public proof: paper/typical_intersections.tex, typical:main,
typical:block-measure and typical:canonical-measure.
Review: audit/v4/final_typical_review.txt.

3. UNIFORM PARAMETER GEOMETRY (SUPPORTING PART OF THE SAME A03 SECTION)

Prior results: Abram--Lagarias (2014), Theorems 2.3 and 5.5, supply the
exact dimensions for the positive comparison family 1+3^k. The integer
golden-ratio coding precedent is Abram--Bolshakov--Lagarias, Intersections
of multiplicative translates of 3-adic Cantor sets II: Two infinite
families, Theorem 6.2, with the identity multiplier excluded.
https://arxiv.org/html/1508.05967v2

Included extension: a causal isometric embedding for arbitrary nonidentity
3-adic units gives the sharp Assouad bound log_3(phi), where
phi=(1+sqrt(5))/2. The families 1+3^k and 1-3^k give the exact punctured
upper and lower dimension limits at the identity. These consequences
complete the parameter section; they are not counted as additional
independently resolved numbered questions.

Public proof: paper/typical_intersections.tex, typical:golden-gap and
typical:oscillation. Prior integer bounds and the positive comparison
family are attributed explicitly; the identity lambda=1 is not put under
the smaller golden-ratio bound.

4. FRACTIONAL-PART CIRCLE QUESTION AND FIXED MOVING TARGETS (L18)

Primary source: J. C. Lagarias, Ternary expansions of powers of 2,
arXiv:math/0512006v4, Section 5 (p. 26 in that version).
https://arxiv.org/html/math/0512006v4

The source asks about fractional ternary expansions omitting digit 2.
The target is the normalized digits-0,1 Cantor set
  K_01={sum_{j>=1} epsilon_j 3^(-j): epsilon_j in {0,1}}.
This differs from the standard middle-thirds digits-0,2 set K_02=2 K_01.

Included in v4: the set of x on R/Z with {2^n x} in K_01 infinitely
often has Hausdorff and packing dimension zero. A separate scaling/parity
argument proves the same assertion for K_02 as an additional variant.
The K_02 result is not presented as the original question's target.

The same section proves zero Hausdorff and packing dimension for every
fixed real shift sequence in the target Z+K_01, and for every fixed
3-adic shift sequence in the target Sigma. Each sequence is chosen before
the starting point. Any fixed finite number of alternatives at each time
is allowed. No union over all sequences or unbounded number of choices
per time is asserted.

Dependency: the transfer uses the earlier uniform affine finite-word
bound and extraction theorem already contained in the same manuscript.
The new real periodic-target transfer, target conventions, countable
cover and moving-target quantifiers are proved in the new section.

Scope: dense dyadic rationals give the circle sets box dimension one.
Packing dimension zero therefore does not mean a global subpower box
count. The circle class of 1 is 0 and belongs to both exceptional sets;
this does not exclude the ordinary starting point 1 from E_2.

Public proof: paper/circle_targets.tex, circle:main,
circle:cor:dimension and circle:cor:finite-targets.
Review: audit/v4/final_circle_integration_review.txt.

5. VERSION AND COMPANION BOUNDARIES

Earlier real and 3-adic zero-dimension statements, dyadic nesting limits,
generic-base metric results and multiplier-counting results retain their
earlier version attribution. They are not announced again as v4 results.
Earlier reports are preserved with their original reviewed file hashes
and are not represented as a new certification of the whole archive.

The companion ternary-cantor-quotients article remains separately versioned.
New quotient-specific candidates are reserved for its own reviewed update.
This release neither absorbs that article nor certifies its future changes.

Open here: 1 notin E_2; the exact good-power list; a subpolynomial count of
actual good powers; the full exceptional parameter spectrum and category
questions; the sharp quotient counts and stronger counting asymptotics.
No finite computation, restricted arithmetic class or reformulation is
presented as a proof of these statements.
