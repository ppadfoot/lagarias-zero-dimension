A. EXACT MAIN-TEXT CHECK

1. Statement, lines 39–65: the metric, exceptional-set definition, two dimension claims, and countable compact cover conclusion are mutually consistent. The text correctly separates zero dimension from countability and from exclusion of the point 1. Sole author is Timofei Iuzhakov.

2. Sumset estimate, lines 104–134: the specialization h0=1 and N_k=2^k is correct. The stabilizer is that of the actual sum, and the Kneser iteration in the quotient is justified. The constant is B_rk=r log3+2^(k+1)log2.

3. Templates and normalization, lines 136–210: the English statement permits arbitrary ordinary integer unit A_t and arbitrary 3-adic unit remainders u_i. It does not accidentally impose integrality on the latter or positivity on exponent differences n_i−n0. The displayed approximation precision absorbs normalization errors after division by 3^E_i. The finite data determining each compact are countable; the p-adic limiting coefficients are not retained as uncountable covering parameters. d_i=0 is a valid specialization for the original multiplicative orbit.

4. Carry identity, lines 212–240: all r lower residues are ordinary nonnegative representatives, giving carry 0<=e<=r−1. The center depends only on the input strictly before the source j. The output condition applies to the relevant output q-window and does not assume auxiliary A_t X windows are ternary Cantor words.

5. Low-digit removal, lines 242–258: fixing the low b input digits fixes the residue of the weighted sum; the first coefficient u_i is a unit. Its fixed low-part contribution is absorbed into the target shifts. The remaining first high factor is multiplied by u_i modulo 3^k, a bijection. The interval of possible low-target-minus-carry representatives has length <2·3^b, so at most two translates are enough. This matches the specialized Stage 16 argument.

6. Finite W_L and joint letters, lines 260–281: the finite words are allowed to be more numerous than prefixes extending to the simultaneous compact. This is harmless for an upper bound. Since each output prefix lies in Sigma_L, every internal q-block lies in Sigma_q. Multipliers are 3-adic units, including rational negative powers of 2. The joint alphabet has r−1 rows and cardinality Q=3^((r−1)q). Its relations depend only on X_<source. The stated positional disjointness and pair multiplicity C=binom(r,2) follow from the separated ranges of possible distances.

7. Conditional supports and full products, lines 292–323: fixing X_<j+b,F fixes the low b digits of all joint rows. Each required high-row projection is an affine unit image of C_j, hence has |C_j| possibilities. The full joint support belongs to A_j because A_j is determined by X_<j,F. On a good edge, the full Cartesian product belongs to the relation frozen at the single realized source prefix. Therefore supports arising under different endpoint prefixes may indeed be selected independently. The manuscript explicitly states that they need not be jointly realizable by a new input word. The earlier actual-tuple-versus-product pitfall is avoided by the exact theorem used.

8. Incidence and entropy, lines 325–358: all factors r,K,k and the finite span R are correct. Short systems with N<E_i are covered by the missing-incidence term. Conditional entropy chain rule uses the same F throughout. Missing overlap multiplicity is k(b+k−1), giving the stated boundary constant. The deterministic F decomposition of H(X) is valid.

9. Thresholds and limits, lines 360–388: for fixed k,r the displayed power hierarchy makes theta,epsilon_F,eta tend to zero. For each fixed u, each compact has a fixed finite R; L→infinity is taken within that compact. Upper box dimension bounds pass to the countable union by packing dimension's countable stability, not by upper box dimension's stability. The subsequent u→infinity, r→infinity at fixed k, and k→infinity are correctly ordered. The explicit countable compact covering assertion follows by selecting k,r,u successively for any delta>0. Both dimension conclusions, including packing, are supported.
