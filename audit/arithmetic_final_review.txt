MATHEMATICAL CHECKS ON THE EXACT DRAFT

1. Definition and specialization.
Main lines 40–64 define E_2 using infinitely many nonnegative forward
visits, and clearly separate it from countability and the point 1.
The chosen visit exponents n_i are nonnegative, but differences n_i-n_0
may be negative. This causes no problem: every 2^{n_i-n_0} is a 3-adic
unit. The multiplier is not incorrectly treated as an ordinary integer.

The general affine formula of Step 16 specializes to d_i=0 exactly,
since f_n(x)=2^n x and C=Sigma. X=2^{n_0}x, c_i=2^{n_i-n_0}, and
c_iX=2^{n_i}x. All normalized visits are genuine visits. In particular,
the proof does not normalize by a non-visited limiting multiplier.

2. ESS and tail extraction (main lines 135–194).
The coefficients rho^{-1},-a_t are fixed for the chosen point and
subsequence; their possible transcendence over Q does not invalidate
ESS. The vector group <2> x <3>^j has finite rank 1+j. The left-hand
terms have the distinct valuations 0,e_1,...,e_j, so every nonempty
subsum is nonzero. Applying ESS over an algebraic closure of Q_3 and
restricting its solutions back to Q_3 is valid.

The compactness argument successively extracts limits of normalized
unit tails. Exact stops are finite at every fixed finite stage by ESS.
Thus arbitrarily many well-separated tails are obtained, but only a
fixed finite number r is used at once. There is no illegitimate
simultaneous extraction for all r.

For each fixed finite K, integer approximations A_t satisfy the required
finite maximum of accuracy conditions. The further true-visit anchor
alpha_0 satisfies its own finite accuracy maximum. The correction to
c_i^*, and each change a_t -> A_t, has valuation at least E_i+q.
Consequently u_i = u_i^* modulo 3^q, hence u_i remains in Z_3^*.
The draft never assumes u_i is an ordinary integer.

3. Countable covers (main lines 196–209).
The compact K_D is defined using the exact unit multipliers c_i.
Its cover index uses only finite integer data and visit indices.
The possibly uncountable limit coefficients rho,a_t have disappeared
from that index. They establish existence of one admissible finite
system for each x, and do not generate an uncountable union.
The normalizing factor 2^{-n_0} is an isometry of Z_3, preserving the
relevant dimensions and compactness.

4. Exact carry identity (main lines 211–239).
T=3^{E_i}u_iP is the correct d_i=0 specialization of Step 16.
Its ordinary residue t_v lies in [0,3^v), and D_j is integral and
known from X_{<j}. The r lower parts are nonnegative and less than
3^v, so the integer carry is in {0,...,r-1}. Division by 3^v and
reduction modulo 3^q give precisely the displayed relation. Neither
signed A_t nor nonintegral rational/3-adic unit u_i changes this
calculation. Intermediate digits may affect e, but only the bounded
carry e, not the claimed prefix-dependent center D_j.

5. Two-target reduction and capacity (main lines 103–133, 241–257).
After low digits are fixed, the contribution u_i times the low part
of B_0 belongs to the fixed center. The varying high part is multiplied
by u_i modulo 3^k, an invertible map. The interval for y_low-e has
strictly fewer than 2*3^b units of length, so each fixed residue has at
most two representatives. Their high translates are fixed for the
chosen low parts and center. Thus no uncontrolled extra target is lost.

For the sumset lemma, H stabilizes the sum itself. The bound 3^h<=2^{h+1}
implies h<=1. The quotient sum is aperiodic; all its partial sums are
aperiodic, so repeated two-summand Kneser is valid. n_i<=2^{n_i-1}
gives the stated B_{r,k}. The lemma is applied only after obtaining a
full Cartesian product, not merely a collection of realized tuples.

6. Interface with the finite causal lemma (main lines 259–322).
The joint alphabet size Q=3^{(r-1)q} is a valid uniform upper bound.
All edge roles use the indicated joint-window projections. The finite
lemma explicitly permits nonlocal maps Z_j; the only causal requirement
is on the relations, and D_j has the required dependency. Separated
ranges of positive distances give the claimed positional codegree and
future-position disjointness. Conditioning on X_{<j+b} fixes the low
parts of each auxiliary window. Unit multiplication is then an affine
bijection on the remaining high block, so all required projection
sizes equal |C_j|. Prefix consistency of assigned alphabets is what
allows all those conditional supports inside their assigned palettes.

7. Entropy and limits (main lines 324–387).
I read and checked these steps as a cross-check of the arithmetic
interface. Each template role misses min(E_i,N) positions. Summed
incidence error is bounded by r*k*log(3)*K*R. The window chain rule and
missing multiplicities give the displayed boundary term. Parameter
choices force theta, epsilon_F and eta to zero at fixed k,r. Each
finite system has fixed R before L tends to infinity. Taking countable
compact covers uses packing, not raw upper box, dimension. Sending
r to infinity at fixed k and then k to infinity yields both zero
conclusions, conditional on the established finite lemma.
